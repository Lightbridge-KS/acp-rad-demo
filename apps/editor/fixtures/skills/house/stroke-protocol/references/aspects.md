# ASPECTS — Alberta Stroke Program Early CT Score

Reference material for `/stroke-protocol`. Synthetic teaching text, not a clinical authority.

10 points, one deducted for each region showing early ischemic change on a non-contrast CT of
the brain. The score is only meaningful for **middle cerebral artery** territory infarction.

## Regions

| Level | Regions |
|---|---|
| Ganglionic (basal ganglia) | caudate · lentiform nucleus · internal capsule · insular ribbon · M1 · M2 · M3 |
| Supraganglionic | M4 · M5 · M6 |

`M1`–`M3` are the anterior, lateral and posterior MCA cortex at the ganglionic level; `M4`–`M6`
are the same three territories immediately above it.

## Scoring

Start at 10. Deduct 1 for each region with early ischemic change — parenchymal hypoattenuation
or loss of grey–white differentiation. Deduct only for **established** change; a normal-looking
region in a patient scanned within the hour is scored as normal.

- **8–10** — small or no established core.
- **4–7** — moderate.
- **0–3** — large established infarct.

## Worked example

A study showing loss of the left insular ribbon and hypoattenuation of the left lentiform
nucleus, with everything else normal, scores **8/10** — two regions deducted. Report it as:

> - Early ischemic change in the left insular ribbon and lentiform nucleus, ASPECTS 8/10, in
>   keeping with acute infarction of the left middle cerebral artery territory.

A hyperdense left M1 segment alongside it is stated as its own item, naming the vessel and side.
