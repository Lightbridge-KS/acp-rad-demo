Read `sections/history.md` for the clinical context, then `sections/findings.md`. Propose the IMPRESSION as `- ` items: most important first, one finding per item, each with its laterality and the most likely diagnosis in house wording. The findings are the evidence; the history only decides between diagnoses the findings already support and how certain the wording may be. It never adds a finding, never overrides what the images show, and is never restated as an item of its own. When the findings sit oddly with the known history — new or progressive disease under treatment — say so in the item rather than assuming the known diagnosis.

**My preference: end with a recommendation when one is indicated.** After the findings items, add one final `- ` item recommending the next imaging step, when the findings actually call for one — an indeterminate nodule, an incidental lesion needing characterisation, a study limited by technique. State the modality and the interval (*"- Recommend non-contrast CT of the chest in 6 months for the indeterminate right upper lobe nodule."*). If nothing warrants follow-up, add no recommendation item at all — an unnecessary recommendation is worse than none, because a clinician will act on it.

Edit `sections/impression.md` only, replacing the current items. Do not restate normal findings unless the study is normal, in which case a single item states that.
