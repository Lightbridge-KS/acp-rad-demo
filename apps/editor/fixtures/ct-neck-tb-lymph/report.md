**TECHNIQUES**: Plain and contrast enhanced axial helical scan of the neck and sinuses with 2.5-mm (soft tissue) and 1.25-mm (bone) slice thickness with multiplanar reformations.
**Intravenous contrast brand, concentration, volume**: Ultravist370; 60 ml.
**Immediate complication**: None.
**COMPARISON**: None.

**FINDINGS**:
**Included brain parenchyma:** The visualized brain parenchyma shows normal grey and white matter differentiation. No acute large territorial infarction or intraparenchymal hemorrhage is identified. There is no abnormal enhancing intracranial lesion or leptomeningeal enhancement detected.
**Lymph nodes**:
- Multiple enlarged, matted heterogeneous enhancing lymph nodes with central necrosis along the bilateral cervical level IIB, III, right cervical level VA/B, and bilateral supraclavicular region, size up to 3.7 cm at the left cervical level IIB/III.
- Several other lymph nodes along the right cervical level IIA, left cervical IIB and VA, up to 1.2 cm in long axis diameter; probably reactive nodes.
