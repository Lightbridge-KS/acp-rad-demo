**MDCT SCAN OF THE CHEST**

**HISTORY:** Chronic cough for 2 months.
**TECHNIQUES:** Contrast-enhanced axial CT scan of the chest was performed with a slice thickness of 2.5 mm. Coronal and sagittal reformations were done.
**COMPARISON:** None.

**FINDINGS:**
**Tubes and lines:** None.
**Lung parenchyma:** A 6-mm solid nodule at the posterior segment of the right upper lobe. No consolidation or tree-in-bud opacity.
**Central airways:** Patent trachea and bilateral main bronchi.
**Pleural cavity:** No pneumothorax or pleural effusion.
**Heart:** Normal heart size. No pericardial effusion.
**Vessels:** Normal caliber of the thoracic aorta. Mild atherosclerotic change of the aorta.
**Mediastinum and hilar:** No mediastinal or hilar lymphadenopathy.
**Chest wall and lower neck:** Unremarkable.
**Visualized upper abdomen:** Unremarkable.
**Bony structures:** Degenerative changes of the spine.

**IMPRESSION:**
- A 6-mm solid nodule at the right upper lobe, indeterminate; follow-up CT in 12 months is suggested.
