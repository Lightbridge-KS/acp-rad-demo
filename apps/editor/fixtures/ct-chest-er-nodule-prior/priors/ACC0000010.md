**MDCT SCAN OF THE WHOLE ABDOMEN**

**HISTORY:** Right upper quadrant pain for 2 days.
**TECHNIQUES:** Contrast-enhanced axial CT scan of the whole abdomen was performed with a slice thickness of 2.5 mm. Coronal and sagittal reformations were done.
**COMPARISON:** None.

**FINDINGS:**
**Lung bases:** No nodule, consolidation or pleural effusion at the visualized lung bases.
**Liver:** Normal size and attenuation. No focal lesion.
**Biliary system:** No biliary dilatation.
**Gallbladder:** Two small calcified stones without wall thickening or pericholecystic fluid.
**Spleen, pancreas and adrenal glands:** Unremarkable.
**Kidneys:** No stone, hydronephrosis or solid mass.
**Bowel:** No dilatation or wall thickening.
**Peritoneum:** No free fluid.
**Bony structures:** Degenerative changes of the spine.

**IMPRESSION:**
- Cholelithiasis without evidence of acute cholecystitis.
